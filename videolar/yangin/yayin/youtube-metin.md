## Description (EN)
A single spark can turn into a fire in seconds. In this 1-minute animation you'll learn how to prevent fires, raise the alarm, use an extinguisher safely (Pull – Aim – Squeeze – Sweep) and evacuate safely.

🔊 This video has multiple audio tracks: English and Türkçe. Choose your language in Settings > Audio track.

⚠️ This video supports, but does not replace, formal workplace safety training. Only use an extinguisher if you are trained, the fire is small and your escape route is clear. Call your local emergency number.
